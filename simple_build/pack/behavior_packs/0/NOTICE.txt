Classroom Tools for Minecraft Education is a modified version of Educator Tools by Shapescape ApS
(https://github.com/ShapescapeMC/Educator-Tools), used under the GNU LGPL v3.0.
It is not made or endorsed by Shapescape. Changes: removed teams, nicknames, assignments,
inventory, health, limitations, world settings, feedback prompts and letter blocks; added
a single teacher, a student picker with Everybody, and time limits for freezes and locks.
